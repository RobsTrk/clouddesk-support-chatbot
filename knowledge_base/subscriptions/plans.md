# CloudDesk subscription changes
Category: subscriptions
Intent: subscription, upgrade, cancellation

## Upgrade
A workspace owner can review available plans and select an upgrade in **Settings → Billing → Plan**. Confirm the owner understands that the change affects the workspace subscription.

## Cancellation
A workspace owner can request cancellation from **Settings → Billing → Plan**. The customer should review the confirmation screen before submitting; Nova must not claim the subscription was cancelled.

## Escalation conditions
Escalate if the billing controls are unavailable, the requester is not an owner, or an account-specific cancellation needs staff action.
