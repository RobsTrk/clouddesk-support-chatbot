# CloudDesk Slack integration
Category: integrations
Intent: integration

## Steps
1. A workspace owner opens **Settings → Integrations → Slack**.
2. Choose **Connect Slack** and approve the requested workspace permissions.
3. Select the CloudDesk inbox and Slack channel to link.

## Escalation conditions
Escalate permission errors, an unavailable Slack workspace, or failures after reconnecting. Do not ask the customer to share Slack tokens.
