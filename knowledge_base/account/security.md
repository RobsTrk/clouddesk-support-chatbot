# Suspicious account activity
Category: security
Intent: account_security

## Problem
A customer reports an unfamiliar sign-in, possible account compromise, or unauthorised access.

## Immediate guidance
Ask the customer to change their CloudDesk password immediately, review workspace members, and avoid sharing passwords or verification codes. Do not ask for their password.

## Restrictions
Nova cannot verify devices, revoke sessions, or make account-security changes.

## Escalation conditions
Create a high-priority human review for every suspected compromise or unknown-device report. Include the customer message and any approximate time of the notification.
