# Reset a CloudDesk password
Category: account
Intent: password_reset

## Problem
You forgot your password or need to reset it.

## Steps
1. Open the CloudDesk sign-in page and choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address for the CloudDesk account.
3. Open the reset message and choose a new unique password.
4. Sign in with the new password.

## Important notes
Reset links are sent only to the account email. Customers using SSO should reset their password through their identity provider.

## Escalation conditions
Escalate if no reset message arrives after checking spam, the link is expired repeatedly, or the customer lost access to the account email.
