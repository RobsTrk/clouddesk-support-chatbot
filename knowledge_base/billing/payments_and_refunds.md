# Failed or duplicate payments and refunds
Category: billing
Intent: failed_payment, refund

## Failed payment
Confirm the payment method is current and that the card issuer permits the charge. A workspace owner can update the payment method in **Settings → Billing**.

## Duplicate charge or refund request
Nova cannot issue refunds or confirm account-specific charges. Collect the invoice identifier, approximate charge date, and workspace email, then create a human-review escalation.

## Restriction
Never promise that a refund has been approved or issued.
