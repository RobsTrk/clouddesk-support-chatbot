# CloudDesk invoices and billing cycles
Category: billing
Intent: billing

## Solution
Workspace owners can find invoices in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Invoices show the billing period, plan, and payment status. CloudDesk subscriptions renew on the billing cycle selected for the workspace.

## Escalation conditions
Escalate billing disputes, missing invoices that a workspace owner cannot access, and requests requiring account-specific payment information.
